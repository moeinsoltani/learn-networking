# Phase 6: Cross-Cutting Quality Attributes

Some qualities can't be assigned to one component — they're properties of the whole
system, and they must be *designed in* rather than bolted on after an incident.
This phase gives each of the big cross-cutting attributes its own architectural
treatment: scalability and performance, security (built into the structure, not
around it), observability (so you can operate what you built), API design (the most
expensive contract to change), and cloud/deployment architecture, where
deployability itself has become a first-class quality attribute.
