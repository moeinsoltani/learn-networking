# Phase 3: Architectural Styles

Styles are the reusable shapes of whole systems, each optimizing for a different
quality at the expense of others. This phase works through the ones you'll actually
choose among: the (underrated) monolith and its modular form, the "keep the domain
independent" family (layered, hexagonal, clean), microservices with an honest
accounting of their cost, event-driven architecture, and the granularity question
that decides whether a decomposition succeeds or becomes a distributed monolith.
