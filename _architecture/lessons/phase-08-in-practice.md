# Phase 8: The Architect in Practice

The last phase is about the parts of the job that no diagram captures: deciding
whether to build at all, choosing technology with judgment instead of hype, getting
an architecture *adopted* by teams you don't command, and recognizing the classic
failure modes by name before they bite. It ends with a capstone that puts the whole
track together — a real product brief taken from requirements to a defended,
documented, evaluated architecture.
