# Phase 1: The Architect's Role & Mindset

Before any diagram, the shift is mental. A senior developer optimizes the code in
front of them; an architect optimizes the *system* and the decisions that shape
it — most of which are about qualities (scalability, security, changeability), not
features. This phase rewires how you think: architecture is the set of decisions
that are significant and hard to reverse, everything is a trade-off, and the job is
to make those trade-offs explicit and driven by the small number of requirements
that actually matter.
